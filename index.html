QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Need more detail? Send us your question and our team can guide you.

How can I enquire about an Umrah or Hajj package?

Use the travel enquiry form below, select Hajj or Umrah, and include your intended dates, number of travellers and any preferences. Our team can then discuss current options.

Can I ask about Makkah and Madinah hotels?

Yes. Visit the hotel directory or use the enquiry form to share your dates, room requirements and preferred location. Rates and availability need to be reconfirmed before booking.

Can I request flight tickets, visa assistance or transport?

Yes. Select the relevant service in the enquiry form and add your route, dates or requirements. The website form sends your request to WhatsApp; it does not itself issue tickets or visas.

Does submitting an enquiry confirm my booking?

No. An enquiry is a request for information only. Your booking is confirmed only after your team verifies availability, pricing, terms and required documents with you.
