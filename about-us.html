Our Story

Travel planning with a personal touch

Meeqan Travel & Tours helps individuals, families and groups explore travel options with clear communication and practical support. We coordinate enquiries for Umrah travel, accommodation, transport and flights, tailoring recommendations to your dates and preferences.

Here to help you plan with confidence

Every journey begins with understanding what matters to you: dates, group size, comfort, budget and the kind of support you need. Our team helps you compare suitable options and understand the next steps before you confirm a booking.

We aim to make travel planning more organised by bringing related enquiries—hotels, flights, ground transport and visa guidance—into one conversation.
